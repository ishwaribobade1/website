What is Agentic AI ?


Agentic AI refers to intelligent systems that do not just answer questions or generate content. Instead of waiting for step-by-step human commands, these systems autonomously set goals, create multi-step plans, utilize external tools (like APIs or databases), and take action to solve complex problems with minimal human supervision. Agentic AI is an artificial intelligence system that can accomplish a specific goal with limited supervision. It consists of AI agents—machine learning models that mimic human decision-making to solve problems in real timeb. In a multiagent system, each agent performs a specific subtask required to reach the goal and their efforts are coordinated through AI orchestration.
