What is Gemini ?


Gemini is a family of generative artificial intelligence (AI) models and an intelligent virtual assistant developed by Google. It serves as a comprehensive productivity tool that can understand and generate text, computer code, images, audio, and video simultaneously. Multimodal Processing: Unlike older AI models designed for just text, Gemini is built from the ground up to natively process multiple types of media—meaning it can analyze a video, summarize a complex PDF, or generate a song.Google Ecosystem Integration: Gemini connects directly with your everyday workspace tools like Gmail, Docs, Drive, and Flights, allowing you to quickly surface information, draft emails, or plan trips across apps.Real-Time Search: Because it is built by Google, it utilizes real-world information from Google Search to provide up-to-date and highly contextualized answers.Advanced Features: It features interactive tools like Gemini Live (for natural voice conversations), Gems (custom, role-specific chatbots), and Canvas (a workspace for coding and creative editing).
